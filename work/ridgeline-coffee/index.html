Our Story

Started with one roaster and a lot of stubbornness.

We wanted the kind of coffee you'd drive an hour for, without the hour-long drive. So we bought a little roaster, learned the hard way, and opened our doors on Main Street.

Every bag is roasted in-house in small batches, and every pastry is made before sunrise.
