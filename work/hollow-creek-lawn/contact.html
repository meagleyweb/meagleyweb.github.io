FAQ

Good questions.

Do I have to sign a contract?+

Nope. Weekly and every-other-week service is month to month. Pause for vacation or cancel any time with a text.

Do I need to be home?+

No. Just leave the gate unlocked. We'll text you when we're done, with a photo.

What if it rains on my day?+

We'll move you to the next dry day and send you a heads-up text. You're never charged for a skipped visit.

How do I pay?+

You'll get a link after each visit to pay by card, or you can set up autopay. Cash and check are fine too.

Do you haul off clippings and leaves?+

Clippings are mulched back into the lawn because it's better for the grass. Leaves and limbs from cleanups are hauled away.
