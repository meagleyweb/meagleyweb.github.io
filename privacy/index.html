Privacy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Meagley Web Design is run by Jacob Meagley. This page covers what I collect through meagley.tech and what I do with it. I don't sell your information or share it for anyone else's advertising.

What you send me

When you fill out the contact form or the questionnaire, I get what you typed, such as your name, business, phone, email, links and what you'd like on your site. If you text or email me, I get your message and anything you attach.

I use it to reply, prepare your preview or quote, and do the work you ask for.

What the site counts

I count visits so I know whether the site is working. The count includes the page, the date, the website that sent you here, and whether a form was sent. It doesn't use cookies.

To avoid counting you twice in a day, your IP address and browser type are turned into a scrambled code. I don't store the IP address, and the code is removed after the day ends, when the next visit is counted. If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, you aren't counted.

What stays on your device

Your browser remembers whether you chose light or dark mode. The questionnaire also saves your answers on your device so you don't lose them, and clears them after you send it. I can't see saved answers you haven't sent. You can remove both by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Companies that help run this

These companies handle information for the site under their own privacy policies:

  • Cloudflare hosts the site, stores the visit counts and forwards my email.
  • Web3Forms delivers the contact form and questionnaire to my email.
  • Google provides my inbox and the site's fonts. Loading the fonts shares your IP address with Google. If you came from a Google ad, Google also has its own record of that click.
  • Resend sends the emails I write from my meagley.tech address.
  • GitHub stores the site's files and still serves a copy at its old address.

I may also share information if the law requires it.

How long I keep it

I keep messages and project details while we're talking or working together, and afterward for my business records. Daily visit totals don't identify anyone, so I may keep them.

Your choices

You can ask what I have about you, correct it, or ask me to delete it. Email jacob@meagley.tech or text 803-509-1640. I may need to keep some records, such as signed agreements and payments.

Other websites

This page is only about meagley.tech. Websites I build for clients belong to those clients, and they decide how information is used there.

Children

This site is for business owners. I don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
