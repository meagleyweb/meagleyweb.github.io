Set up your hosting account

Your website will live in your own Cloudflare account, so it always belongs to you. This takes about 10 minutes. If you'd like, we can do it together.

  1. Create your account

    Go to dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up and sign up with an email you'll always have. Then open the email they send you and verify it.

    Keep your password to yourself. I never need it.

  2. Protect it

    In your profile, turn on two-factor authentication. Your domain and website will be in this account, so it's worth the extra minute.

  3. Get your domain

    Already own one? Skip this step and text me what it is.

    If not, go to the Register domains page and search for the name you want. Domains start at $11.99 a year. Not every domain is available and some cost more, so the price may vary.

    Check the spelling, pick how many years you want, and buy it with your own card. Leave auto-renew on so you don't lose it. Watch for an email asking you to verify your contact email, and do that right away or the domain can be put on hold.

  4. Invite me

    • Open the Members page and choose Invite members.
    • Enter jacob@meagley.tech.
    • Create a permission policy for the entire account, with the role Administrator.
    • Choose Invite members to send it.

    This lets me build and run your site. It doesn't let me manage your billing profile or your members, and you can remove me anytime from the same page.

  5. Tell me you're done

    Text me, and I'll take it from there.

If a button looks different

Cloudflare changes its pages sometimes. Text me a screenshot and I'll point you to the right spot. Just don't include your password, card number or any login codes.
